Account & Data Deletion

Request Account Deletion

If you want to delete your MessClub account and associated personal data, you can submit an account deletion request using the contact information provided below.

How to request deletion:

Send us a deletion request from the email address associated with your MessClub account. Please include your registered email address or phone number so that we can identify your account.

Information to Include

Please include the following information in your request:

  • Your registered name
  • Your registered email address
  • Your registered phone number
  • Subject: MessClub Account Deletion Request

What Happens After Your Request?

After receiving your request, we will verify the request and process the deletion of your MessClub account and applicable associated personal data.

Some information may be retained when required by law, for legitimate security purposes, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, or financial and transaction record requirements.

Data That May Be Deleted

Depending on the account and applicable requirements, account deletion may include deletion of information associated with your MessClub account, such as:

  • Account profile information
  • Name and contact information
  • Login-related account data
  • Saved preferences
  • Other personal information associated with your account

Certain records related to completed transactions, payments, subscriptions, bookings, security, or legal obligations may be retained where required or permitted by applicable law.

Contact Us

For account deletion requests, please contact the MessClub team:

Email: messclubteam@gmail.com

Important

Account deletion is permanent once processed. You may lose access to your MessClub account, subscriptions, bookings, saved information, and other account-related features.
