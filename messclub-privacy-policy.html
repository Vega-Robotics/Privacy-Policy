Privacy Policy for MessClub

Last Updated: September 10, 2026

MessClub ("MessClub", "we", "us", or "our") operates the MessClub mobile application and related services (collectively, the "Service"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, protect, and share information when you use MessClub.

By using MessClub, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy, to the extent permitted by applicable law.

1. Information We Collect

A. Account Information

When you create or maintain an account, we may collect:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Gender, where provided
  • Occupation, where provided
  • Password or authentication information
  • Account type, such as Customer or Hotel
  • Hotel or business information, where applicable

B. Meal Subscription and Reservation Information

When a customer uses MessClub's meal plans, trial service, reservations, QR-based hotel access, or meal attendance features, we may collect:

  • Selected hotel or mess
  • Subscription plan and duration
  • Number of meals allocated, reserved, consumed, released, and remaining
  • Meal booking, reservation, ticket, and attendance information
  • Meal date and meal type, such as lunch or dinner
  • Subscription start and meal-balance status
  • Transaction or payment status, when applicable
  • Information needed to prevent duplicate or unauthorized meal usage

C. Payment Information

MessClub's payment functionality may be temporarily unavailable while live payment processing is being prepared. During a period when payments are disabled, MessClub does not intentionally process a live subscription payment through the application and does not create a successful payment transaction merely because a user selects a payment option.

When live payments are enabled in the future, payments may be processed through a third-party payment provider such as Razorpay. The applicable provider may collect and process payment information according to its own privacy policy and security practices.

MessClub does not intentionally store complete debit card numbers, credit card numbers, CVV codes, or banking credentials on its own servers.

Razorpay Privacy Policy: https://razorpay.com/privacy-policy/

D. Email, Phone Verification, and CAPTCHA Information

Email and phone number may be collected as part of account information. At present, email verification and phone OTP verification may be disabled while these features are being prepared or updated. If these verification features are enabled in the future, related verification information may be processed for authentication and security purposes.

MessClub may use CAPTCHA or similar security verification to help confirm that requests are made by legitimate users and to reduce automated abuse. CAPTCHA challenges, verification results, and related technical information may be processed for a limited period for security purposes.

Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

E. Hotel Information

If you register or operate a hotel/mess through MessClub, we may collect information such as:

  • Hotel or mess name
  • Business address
  • Contact information
  • Menu information
  • Meal pricing
  • Subscription-related hotel eligibility information
  • Business documents or verification information, where required
  • Hotel images and other information provided by the hotel

F. Hotel View and Usage Statistics

When a customer opens a hotel's details page, MessClub may record an aggregated hotel view. To reduce repeated or artificial counting, the Service may check whether the same customer or session has already viewed that hotel during the same day.

Hotel owners may receive aggregated statistics such as daily, weekly, and monthly hotel views. These statistics are intended to show overall interest in a hotel and do not provide hotel owners with individual customer browsing histories.

We do not intentionally provide hotel owners with a customer's personal contact information merely because the customer viewed a hotel.

G. Security and Abuse-Prevention Information

To protect MessClub and its users, we may process limited technical and security information, which may include:

  • IP address
  • Device or browser information
  • User-agent information
  • Request frequency and rate-limiting information
  • Failed login attempts
  • CAPTCHA failures
  • Registration and authentication security events
  • Suspicious or potentially abusive activity

This information may be used to detect and prevent automated abuse, account attacks, fraudulent activity, unauthorized access, and misuse of the Service.

H. Information You Provide

We may also collect information that you voluntarily provide when you:

  • Contact customer support
  • Submit feedback
  • Report a problem
  • Make a request
  • Communicate with us
  • Update your profile

2. How We Use Your Information

We may use collected information to:

  • Create and manage user accounts
  • Authenticate users and maintain account security
  • Provide CAPTCHA and other anti-abuse protections
  • Apply rate limits and login-attempt protections
  • Detect suspicious activity and prevent fraud or misuse
  • Manage meal subscriptions and trial services
  • Track allocated, reserved, consumed, released, and remaining meals
  • Manage hotel reservations, QR-based access, tickets, and attendance
  • Connect customers with approved hotels and messes
  • Provide hotels with aggregated hotel-view statistics
  • Manage hotel accounts and listings
  • Process payments when live payment functionality is enabled
  • Provide customer support
  • Improve the functionality and performance of MessClub
  • Maintain security and service reliability
  • Communicate important service-related information
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations

3. Information Sharing

We may share information with trusted third-party service providers when necessary to provide, secure, maintain, or improve MessClub.

Payment Providers

When live payments are enabled, payment information may be processed by third-party payment providers such as Razorpay. When payment processing is disabled, the application does not intentionally send a payment to a live payment gateway merely because a user selects the payment option.

Email and Infrastructure Providers

We may use email, cloud hosting, database, authentication, security, and infrastructure providers to operate the Service. These providers may process information as necessary to provide their services.

Hotels

MessClub may provide hotels with information necessary to operate meal reservations and attendance. Hotel owners may also receive aggregated hotel-view statistics. Individual customer browsing information is not intentionally provided to hotels merely because a customer views a hotel.

We do not sell users' personal information.

We may disclose information when required by law, legal process, government request, or when necessary to protect the rights, safety, security, or property of MessClub, our users, or others.

4. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect user information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

Depending on the information and service involved, security measures may include:

  • Password hashing
  • Authentication and authorization controls
  • HTTPS/TLS encryption
  • Rate limiting
  • Login-attempt protection
  • CAPTCHA and automated-abuse protection
  • Access controls
  • Secure database practices
  • Authentication tokens
  • Security and audit logging
  • Protection against unauthorized access and suspicious activity

However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

5. Data Retention and Backups

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary to:

  • Provide our services
  • Maintain user accounts
  • Process and manage subscriptions
  • Maintain meal, reservation, attendance, and hotel records
  • Meet legal, accounting, or regulatory requirements
  • Resolve disputes
  • Prevent fraud and abuse
  • Enforce our agreements

MessClub may maintain secure database backups or exports for operational recovery, business continuity, security, accounting, and disaster recovery purposes. Backup copies may remain for a limited period after information is removed from active systems and may be subject to appropriate access controls.

When information is no longer required, we may delete or anonymize it in accordance with our data retention practices and applicable law.

6. Account Deletion

Users may request deletion of their MessClub account and associated personal information through the account-deletion option available in the application, where provided, or by contacting us.

Important: Account deletion is intended to be permanent. After confirmed deletion, the account may not be recoverable.

Before completing an account deletion request, MessClub may require authentication or another security confirmation to help prevent unauthorized deletion.

When an account is deleted, we will delete or anonymize applicable personal information, subject to information that we are legally required or permitted to retain, including certain financial, accounting, security, audit, or transaction records.

Contact for account deletion or privacy requests: messclubteam@gmail.com

7. Children's Privacy

MessClub does not knowingly collect age-related information or intentionally target children. The app may collect basic account information such as name, email address, phone number, gender, and occupation for account and service-related purposes. We do not knowingly collect information specifically from children under the age of 13.

If you believe that a child under the age of 13 has provided personal information through MessClub without appropriate consent, please contact us at messclubteam@gmail.com. Upon receiving a valid request, we will take reasonable steps to review and delete the information where required by applicable law.

MessClub may use third-party services to operate, secure, and improve the application. These services may include:

  • Payment processing services, when enabled

  • Database services

Third-party providers may collect or process information according to their own privacy policies.

9. Analytics and Hotel View Statistics

MessClub may maintain first-party operational statistics, including aggregated hotel-detail-page views, to help hotels understand overall customer interest and to improve the Service.

Hotel view statistics are intended to be aggregated. We do not intentionally provide hotel owners with individual customer browsing histories or personal contact information solely because a customer viewed a hotel.

Currently, MessClub does not intentionally use third-party analytics or remarketing services unless specifically stated in this Privacy Policy. If such services are added in the future, this Privacy Policy may be updated to explain the information collected and how those services are used.
