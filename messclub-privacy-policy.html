Privacy Policy for MessClub

Last Updated: September 6, 2026

MessClub ("MessClub", "we", "us", or "our") operates the MessClub mobile application and related services (collectively, the "Service"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, protect, and share information when you use MessClub.

By using MessClub, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy, to the extent permitted by applicable law.

1. Information We Collect

A. Account Information

When you create an account, we may collect:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Password or authentication information
  • Account type, such as Customer or Hotel
  • Hotel or business information, where applicable

B. Meal Subscription Information

When a customer purchases or uses a meal subscription, we may collect:

  • Selected hotel or mess
  • Subscription plan
  • Subscription duration
  • Number of meals included
  • Meals used and remaining
  • Meal booking or usage information
  • Subscription start and expiry information
  • Transaction or payment status

C. Payment Information

MessClub allows users to make payments for subscriptions and other services. Payments may be processed through third-party payment providers such as Razorpay.

We do not intentionally store complete debit card numbers, credit card numbers, CVV codes, or banking credentials on our own servers. Payment information is processed by the applicable payment provider according to its privacy policy and security practices.

Razorpay Privacy Policy: https://razorpay.com/privacy-policy/

D. Email and OTP Information

We may send verification emails and one-time passwords (OTPs) to users for account verification, authentication, password recovery, and security purposes.

We may use Gmail SMTP / Google services to deliver these emails.

Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

E. Hotel Information

If you register or operate a hotel/mess through MessClub, we may collect information such as:

  • Hotel or mess name
  • Business address
  • Contact information
  • Menu information
  • Meal pricing
  • Subscription plans
  • Business documents or verification information, where required
  • Hotel images and other information provided by the hotel

F. Information You Provide

We may also collect information that you voluntarily provide when you:

  • Contact customer support
  • Submit feedback
  • Report a problem
  • Make a request
  • Communicate with us
  • Update your profile

2. How We Use Your Information

We may use collected information to:

  • Create and manage user accounts
  • Verify user identity and email addresses
  • Send OTP and authentication emails
  • Process subscriptions and payments
  • Manage meal subscriptions
  • Track available and used meals
  • Connect customers with hotels and messes
  • Manage hotel accounts and listings
  • Provide customer support
  • Prevent fraud, misuse, and unauthorized access
  • Improve the functionality and performance of MessClub
  • Maintain security
  • Communicate important service-related information
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations

Payment Providers

Payment information may be processed by third-party payment providers such as Razorpay.

Email Service Providers

Hosting and Infrastructure Providers

Our application may use third-party hosting, database, cloud, or infrastructure services to operate the Service.

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect user information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

Depending on the information and service involved, security measures may include:

  • Password hashing
  • Authentication and authorization controls
  • Secure API communication
  • HTTPS/TLS encryption
  • Access controls
  • Secure database practices
  • Authentication tokens
  • Protection against unauthorized access

However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

5. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to:

  • Provide our services
  • Maintain user accounts

  • Meet legal, accounting, or regulatory requirements
  • Resolve disputes

  • Enforce our agreements

When information is no longer required, we may delete or anonymize it in accordance with our data retention practices and applicable law.

Users may request deletion of their MessClub account and associated personal information.

Users can request account deletion by contacting us at messclub.contact@gmail.com.

When an account deletion request is received, we will delete or anonymize applicable personal information, subject to information that we are legally required or permitted to retain.

If you believe that a child under the age of 13 has provided personal information through MessClub without appropriate consent, please contact us at messclub.contact@gmail.com. Upon receiving a valid request, we will take reasonable steps to review and delete the information where required by applicable law.
