MessClub Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 21, 2026

Welcome to MessClub. This Privacy Policy explains how MessClub collects, uses, stores, protects, and handles information when you use the MessClub mobile application, website, and related services.

By creating an account or using MessClub, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

Important: MessClub is designed to help customers discover participating messes, subscribe to meal plans, reserve meals, manage meal/token entitlements, share tokens where supported, and interact with participating hotels and mess providers.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use MessClub, we may collect the following categories of information.

1.1 Account Information

When you create a MessClub account, we may collect:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Mobile phone number
  • Gender, where provided
  • Occupation, where provided
  • Password in securely protected form
  • Profile information and profile photo, where provided
  • Account role and account status

1.2 Email Verification and OTP Information

MessClub uses email verification to help verify ownership of the email address associated with an account.

When email verification is used, we may process:

  • Email address
  • One-time password (OTP) or verification code
  • OTP creation and expiration time
  • Email verification status
  • Limited security information associated with the verification attempt
Email OTPs are temporary verification credentials. They are not intended to be used as permanent account passwords.

1.3 Address and Location Information

Depending on the features you use, MessClub may collect address or location-related information that you provide.

This may include:

  • Address
  • Area/locality
  • City
  • Location information associated with participating hotels or messes
  • Location information you provide while searching for hotels

MessClub may use location and locality information to help customers discover participating hotels or messes in a selected area, such as searching for hotels in Katraj, Swargate, or another locality.

1.4 Subscription and Token Information

When you purchase or use a MessClub subscription, we may store information necessary to manage your subscription and meal/token entitlement.

This may include:

  • Selected subscription plan
  • Subscription start and end dates
  • Subscription status
  • Token entitlement and remaining token balance
  • Token usage
  • Token sharing and receiving history
  • Token-related transaction records
  • Trial or promotional entitlement information

Tokens are used within the MessClub platform according to the applicable subscription and reservation rules.

1.5 Hotel and Meal Reservation Information

When you browse, select, or reserve meals through MessClub, we may collect and store information related to those activities.

This may include:

  • Selected hotel or mess
  • Meal type such as lunch or dinner
  • Reservation date and time
  • Reservation status
  • Ticket information
  • Meal served/attendance status
  • Token usage associated with the reservation
  • Cancellation or expiration information

This information is required to operate reservations, tickets, meal servicing, token accounting, hotel operations, and related customer support functions.

1.6 Hotel Search Information

MessClub allows customers to discover participating hotels and messes using information such as hotel names, locations, areas, and localities.

Searches may be processed to return relevant participating hotels. Search information may be associated with technical logs where necessary for security, troubleshooting, or service improvement.

1.7 Notifications

MessClub may send notifications relating to your account and use of the platform.

Notifications may relate to:

  • Reservations
  • Meal availability
  • Reservation status
  • Meal serving status
  • Token sharing
  • Token acceptance
  • Subscription information
  • Referral rewards
  • Security-related events
  • Important service announcements

Notification records may be automatically removed after the applicable notification retention period. This retention process applies only to notification records and does not automatically delete reservations, tickets, subscriptions, payment records, audit records, or other historical account information.

1.8 Referral Information

MessClub may provide a referral system that allows customers to invite other users to join the platform.

If you participate in the referral system, we may process:

  • Your referral code
  • The fact that another user registered using your referral code
  • Referral milestone information
  • Referral reward information
  • Referral token rewards
  • Referral-related timestamps and records

Referral information may be used to calculate and provide applicable rewards according to the MessClub referral program.

1.9 Photos and User-Provided Content

If you choose to upload a profile photo or other supported content, MessClub may process and store that content for the purpose for which it was provided.

1.10 Device and Technical Information

We may automatically collect limited technical information when you use MessClub.

This may include:

  • Device type
  • Operating system
  • Application version
  • Device identifiers where required for security or notifications
  • Push notification token
  • IP address
  • Authentication/session information
  • Technical logs
  • Error and diagnostic information

1.11 Security and Abuse-Prevention Information

To protect users and the platform, MessClub may process technical information related to security and abuse prevention.

This may include:

  • IP address
  • Device-related identifiers
  • Login attempt information
  • Registration attempt information
  • Rate-limit information
  • Authentication events
  • Suspicious activity indicators
  • Security and audit logs

These records may be used to detect fraud, abuse, unauthorized access, automated attacks, and other security threats.

2. How We Use Information

MessClub may use collected information for purposes including:

  • Creating and managing user accounts
  • Verifying email addresses through OTP verification
  • Authenticating users
  • Providing subscription services
  • Managing token balances and token usage
  • Processing hotel and meal reservations
  • Managing meal tickets and serving status
  • Facilitating token sharing
  • Operating referral programs
  • Showing participating hotels and messes
  • Supporting location-based hotel discovery
  • Sending relevant notifications
  • Providing customer support
  • Preventing fraud and abuse
  • Maintaining platform security
  • Monitoring system performance
  • Maintaining operational and audit records
  • Improving the MessClub platform
  • Complying with applicable legal requirements

3. Email OTP Verification

MessClub uses email OTP verification to help confirm that the email address associated with an account belongs to the user.

OTPs are generated for a limited period and are intended to expire after their applicable validity period.

MessClub may maintain limited records of verification attempts for security, abuse prevention, troubleshooting, and audit purposes.

4. How We Handle Passwords

Passwords are not intended to be stored in plain text. Where account passwords are used, they should be securely hashed using appropriate security mechanisms.

Users should never share their password or OTP with another person.

5. Hotel and Mess Information

MessClub acts as a platform connecting customers with participating hotels and mess providers.

Participating hotels may provide information such as:

  • Hotel/mess name
  • Location
  • Address
  • Meal availability
  • Meal timings
  • Meal/menu information
  • Pricing information
  • Hotel type or classification

Some information may be displayed to customers so they can select hotels and reserve meals.

6. Payments

MessClub may support payment functionality in the future or when payment services are enabled.

When third-party payment providers are used, payment processing may be handled by those providers according to their own privacy policies and terms.

MessClub should not store complete card numbers, CVV numbers, banking passwords, or other sensitive payment credentials unless specifically required and legally permitted.

At times when live payment functionality is disabled, users will not be charged through the disabled payment flow.

7. Third-Party Service Providers

MessClub may use third-party service providers to operate certain technical features of the platform.

Depending on the implementation, these services may include providers for:

  • Cloud hosting
  • Database infrastructure
  • Image/file storage
  • Push notifications
  • Email delivery and OTP delivery
  • Payment processing, when enabled
  • Application monitoring and diagnostics

Such providers may process information only as necessary to provide their services and according to their applicable terms and privacy policies.

8. Data Sharing

MessClub does not sell users' personal information.

Information may be shared or made available when reasonably necessary to provide the service.

This may include:

  • Participating hotels/messes for reservation and meal servicing
  • Technical service providers
  • Payment providers when payment functionality is enabled
  • Security and fraud-prevention providers where applicable
  • Government authorities when legally required
  • Professional advisers where legally necessary

We aim to limit shared information to what is reasonably necessary for the relevant purpose.

9. Information Available to Hotels

When you reserve a meal at a participating hotel, the hotel may receive information necessary to identify and serve the reservation.

This may include information such as:

  • Customer name
  • Reservation details
  • Meal type
  • Reservation date/time
  • Ticket or reservation identifier
  • Serving status

Hotels should use customer information only for legitimate operational purposes related to the MessClub service.

10. Hotel Analytics

MessClub may provide participating hotels with aggregate analytics related to their platform presence.

For example, a hotel may receive information about:

  • Number of views
  • Daily views
  • Weekly views
  • Monthly views

These analytics are intended to provide business insights and are not intended to expose individual customers' private browsing histories or unrelated personal information.

11. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain security, resolve disputes, comply with legal obligations, and maintain appropriate business and audit records.

Different categories of information may have different retention periods.

Notification Retention

Notification records may be automatically deleted after the configured notification retention period, such as seven days.

Automatic notification cleanup applies only to notification records. It does not mean that MessClub automatically deletes historical reservations, tickets, subscriptions, token records, payment records, hotel payout records, audit records, or other required information.

12. Data Security

MessClub takes reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information against unauthorized access, misuse, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

Security measures may include:

  • Password hashing
  • Authentication and authorization
  • Email OTP verification
  • Rate limiting
  • Login protection
  • Registration abuse prevention
  • Access controls
  • Server-side validation
  • Security logging
  • Database security controls
  • Secure communication protocols

However, no internet-based system can guarantee absolute security. Users should also take reasonable steps to protect their account credentials and OTPs.

13. Account Deletion

MessClub provides an account deletion mechanism where applicable.

When an eligible user requests account deletion, MessClub may delete, anonymize, or deactivate personal account information according to the applicable deletion process.

Certain information may need to be retained where required for:

  • Legal compliance
  • Fraud prevention
  • Security
  • Financial records
  • Accounting
  • Dispute resolution
  • Audit requirements
  • Protection of the rights and security of MessClub and its users

14. Children's Privacy

MessClub is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children where such collection is prohibited by applicable law.

Users should meet the applicable age requirements before creating and using an account.

If we become aware that personal information has been collected from a child in circumstances where it should not have been collected, we may take reasonable steps to delete the information.

15. Cookies and Similar Technologies

The MessClub website or related services may use cookies or similar technologies where necessary for authentication, security, functionality, analytics, or service operation.

The mobile application may use locally stored information required for authentication, preferences, session management, and application functionality.

16. Data Accuracy

Users are responsible for providing accurate and up-to-date information when creating or maintaining their account.

If your personal information changes, you should update it through the available MessClub account settings where possible.

17. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on applicable law, users may have rights relating to their personal information, including:

  • Requesting access to personal information
  • Requesting correction of inaccurate information
  • Requesting deletion where legally applicable
  • Withdrawing certain permissions where applicable
  • Managing notification permissions through device settings
  • Requesting information about how personal data is processed

Some requests may be subject to legal, security, contractual, or operational limitations.

18. Changes to This Privacy Policy

MessClub may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the application, services, technology, legal requirements, or business practices.

When changes are made, the "Last Updated" date at the top of this Privacy Policy will be updated.

Continued use of MessClub after an updated Privacy Policy becomes effective may constitute acknowledgment of the updated policy to the extent permitted by applicable law.

If you have questions, concerns, requests, or complaints regarding this Privacy Policy or the handling of your personal information, please contact MessClub through the official contact information provided within the application or on the official MessClub app.
